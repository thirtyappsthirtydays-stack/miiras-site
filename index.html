The problem

Money shapes almost every big decision in life. Yet most of us were never taught how it works, and the tools we have only show part of the picture.

Nobody taught us

Most people were never shown how to manage money, so financial decisions feel confusing and intimidating.

Everything is scattered

Budgeting in one app, investments in another, and no clear view of where it's all heading.

A handover nobody planned for

Cerulli Associates estimates $124 trillion will pass between generations in the US through 2048. Yet many families have never talked about what they own, or whether everyone will be okay. Source

Built on research

We listened to both generations first.

Before building anything, we sat down with parents and adult children to talk about money, family and the future. Miiras is shaped by what they told us.
