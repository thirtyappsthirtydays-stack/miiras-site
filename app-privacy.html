App Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version

  • The Miiras app does not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no recordings and no analytics.
  • Nothing you say while using the app leaves the table.

1. Who we are

Miiras ("Miiras," "we," "us") makes the Miiras conversation cards app for iPhone and Android (the "App"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the App. Our website and waitlist are covered by our separate website privacy policy.

2. What the App does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • It does not record audio, save answers or store anything you say.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • It does not send any information to us or to third parties.

3. Information on your device

While the App is open, it keeps track of which questions you've gone through, so it can show your progress and let you go back to a question. This stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is cleared when the App is closed.

4. App stores

You download the App from Apple's App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about your download and device under their own privacy policies. We don't receive personal information about you from them.

5. Contacting us

If you email us, we'll use your email address only to reply to you. You can ask us to delete your message at any time.

6. Children

The App is designed for adults and their families, and is meant to be used together. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Not professional advice

The App helps families talk. It does not provide medical, legal, financial or mental health advice. For decisions, speak with a qualified professional.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, for example by adding features that save data, we'll update this policy and the date at the top of this page before those changes go live.

9. Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email hello@getmiiras.com.
