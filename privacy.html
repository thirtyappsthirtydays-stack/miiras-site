Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what you type into the waitlist form, plus a few details about how you found us.
  • We use it to tell you about early access and, if you agree, to invite you to a feedback call.
  • We never ask for financial information, and we never contact your family.
  • We don't sell or share your information for advertising.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing hello@getmiiras.com.

1. Who we are

Miiras ("Miiras," "we," "us") is an early-stage product that helps families talk about money. This policy explains how we handle information collected through our website, getmiiras.com (the "Site"), including our waitlist.

Miiras has not launched yet. When the Miiras app becomes available, it will have its own privacy policy, which we'll share before you use it.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you join the waitlist, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • Your first name, if you choose to provide it
  • Your answers to the questions on the form, such as who you'd like to talk with and whether you'd like to join a feedback call

Information collected when you sign up. Along with your form answers, we record:

  • Which version of our homepage headline you saw
  • How you arrived at the Site, if the link you followed included a campaign tag (for example, a link we shared on LinkedIn)

Technical information. Like most websites, the services that host and run the Site may automatically receive your IP address, browser type and device information when you visit. See section 4.

What we don't collect. We do not ask for, and you should not send us, financial account details, Social Security numbers, health information, or information about your family members.

3. How we use your information

  • To send you updates about Miiras and early access
  • To contact you about a feedback call, if you said yes
  • To understand which messages and channels bring people to Miiras, so we can improve the Site
  • To respond to messages you send us
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent spam

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Services we use

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the Site. They process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Formspree receives and stores waitlist submissions.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts provides the Site's typefaces. Your browser requests the fonts directly from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Our email provider sends the messages you've asked to receive.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of Miiras or others.

5. Cookies and local storage

The Site does not use cookies or advertising trackers. It saves one small setting in your browser's local storage: the headline version you were shown, so it stays the same when you return. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

Do Not Track. Because we don't track you across other websites, the Site works the same way whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your waitlist information until Miiras launches and for up to 12 months afterward, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. If you unsubscribe, we'll stop emailing you and delete your details unless we need to keep a record that you opted out.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe: every email we send includes a link to stop receiving them.
  • Access, correction and deletion: email us at hello@getmiiras.com to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live in the United States, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we'll honor those requests too. We won't treat you differently for making a request.

8. How we protect your information

The Site uses HTTPS, and we only use providers that take security seriously. We limit access to waitlist information to the people who need it to run Miiras. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, but we work hard to protect what you share with us.

9. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored and processed in the United States by the providers listed above.

10. Children

The Site and waitlist are intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has signed up, please contact us and we'll delete their information.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we'll let people on the waitlist know by email.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email hello@getmiiras.com.

Miiras helps families prepare for conversations about money. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, legal or mental health advice. hello@getmiiras.com
